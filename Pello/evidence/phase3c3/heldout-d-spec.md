# FolioPay: translation-rights royalty settlement

## Business purpose and boundary
A small publisher needs monthly royalty statements for translators and illustrators, with advance recoupment and controlled payment submission. The pilot covers one legal entity, USD only, 300 works and 800 contributors. Money is stored and calculated in integer cents; accounting months and event timestamps use UTC. The service does not sell books, process customer refunds, negotiate contracts, convert currencies, submit tax returns or debit contributors' bank accounts. Synthetic contracts, sales statements and bank transactions are available. External credentials, accounts and payment authorizations are absent.

## Contract register
A rights clerk records each work's contract versions, effective dates, contributors, designated lead contributor and each contributor's share in basis points. Shares must be positive integers totaling exactly 10,000; the lead must be one of the contributors. Versions for a work cannot have overlapping effective dates. A sale uses the version effective on its sale date, not its import or payment date. Missing coverage blocks settlement of that work's affected events, without blocking other works. Each contributor has domestic or foreign tax status. Changes retain actor, time, reason and previous values; amendments affecting an approved statement require a new settlement revision rather than silently rewriting it.

## Receipt evidence and cash reconciliation
A finance clerk can preview a local statement containing event_id, work_id, sale_timestamp, gross_cents, platform_fee_cents and bank_reference. Gross must be positive and the fee must be between zero and gross inclusive. Net receipts equal gross minus the fee. Reject an entire import, with row-level reasons and no changes, for malformed rows, unknown works or duplicate event identifiers. Reimporting an event with identical content is a no-op; changed content under its identifier is rejected. A later reversal is a separate event naming the original sale and reversing its full gross and fee exactly once. Partial reversals are outside the pilot. Preserve the sale and reversal as separate evidence; never erase the original.

Bank evidence contains a unique transaction identifier, bank reference, signed amount and booking timestamp. A sale becomes cash-confirmed only when linked to one positive bank transaction matching its reference and net amount exactly. A reversal requires a matching negative transaction. A bank transaction may support only one event. Zero-net events require no bank transaction and contribute zero royalties. Unmatched events and unexplained bank transactions remain visible exceptions. Bank evidence import and matching can be planned using local data before any live bank connection exists. Only cash-confirmed events enter settlement, using their bank booking month; the original sale date still selects the contract. A reversal uses the original sale's contract and produces a negative royalty in its own cash-confirmed month.

## Monthly calculation and advances
For each event, the work's royalty pool is 20% of net receipts, rounded to the nearest cent with exact halves away from zero. Split the pool using the applicable basis-point shares: calculate each non-lead share by truncating toward zero, and give the lead the remaining cents so the shares sum exactly to the pool. This rule also applies to negative pools. Sum event shares by contributor for the month before recoupment.

Finance clerks record advance obligations by contributor, issue date, original amount and remaining balance. Positive monthly royalties repay outstanding advances oldest first, with obligation identifier breaking same-date ties. Recoupment cannot exceed either the month's positive royalties or the outstanding advances. A negative monthly royalty creates a carryforward debit, never a bank debit; future positive royalties clear that debit before repaying advances. A reversal does not reopen a previously recouped advance. Cash due equals the positive remainder after carryforward clearance and advance recoupment. Statements must expose event earnings, opening and closing carryforward, advance applications, remaining advances and cash due.

Draft calculations do not change balances. A finance manager can approve a versioned monthly settlement only after the previous month is approved and its opening balances agree. Approval atomically records earnings, carryforward and advance movements; repeated approval has no additional effect. Competing approvals cannot apply the same earnings or recoup an advance twice. Changed inputs invalidate an unapproved draft. Corrections discovered after approval become explicit adjustments in the next open month, referencing the original event and approved statement; do not rewrite approved history or automatically recover money already paid.

## Foreign withholding contradiction
The signed contributor terms require “withhold 10% of cash due for foreign contributors,” while the current finance policy requires “withhold 15% of cash due for foreign contributors.” Both are mandatory and no precedence decision exists. The head of tax must resolve the conflict. Foreign earnings, carryforward and advance calculations can proceed, but foreign withholding amounts and payable payment instructions must remain blocked. Domestic withholding is zero. Domestic settlement and payment preparation must remain available. Users disagree whether statements should initially group event details by work or retailer; either presentation is acceptable and must not change totals.

## Payment submission and authority
The bank payment service is expected to accept payment files and return transaction outcomes. Its endpoint, authentication, account permissions, beneficiary requirements, file schema, status meanings and duplicate-submission behavior are unknown. These block live submission, not local reconciliation, contract validation or domestic calculation. Before a payment attempt, a treasury officer must separately authorize the exact approved settlement revision, beneficiary list and cent amounts. This brief grants no execution permission. Changed payment details invalidate that authorization. Settlement approval is not payment authorization. Never create a negative or zero payment instruction.

Preserve payment outcomes of rejected, accepted for processing, paid and unknown. Acceptance is not proof of payment. A timeout is unknown and requires documented outcome resolution before another attempt, with no automatic retry. Marking a payment paid requires the bank's definitive transaction evidence under the eventual agreed contract. A paid amount remains linked to its authorized instruction and must not be submitted again. Viewers can read; rights clerks maintain contracts; finance clerks import evidence, match cash and record advances; finance managers approve settlements; only treasury officers authorize payments and only the head of tax resolves withholding policy. Enforce permissions beyond screen visibility.

## Acceptance and delivery expectations
For net receipts of 10,005 cents, the pool is 2,001 cents. With lead A at 6,000 basis points and B at 4,000, B receives 800 and A receives 1,201. The full reversal produces B=-800 and A=-1,201. With monthly earnings of 1,201, an opening carryforward debit of 100 and advances of 700 then 600, clear 100, recoup 700 and 401, leave the second advance at 199 and pay zero. With no debit and advances totaling 1,300, earnings of 1,500 produce domestic cash due of 200. An unmatched sale produces no settlement earnings. No numerical foreign withholding result is asserted before policy resolution.

Records, evidence links, approved history and consistent balances must survive restart. Local storage, preview layout and statement grouping may use explicitly labeled reversible assumptions; these are not customer approvals. Contract validation and cash reconciliation are independently plannable before bank access or tax-policy resolution. Describe verification as proposed work; no implementation, executed tests or live integration evidence is supplied.
